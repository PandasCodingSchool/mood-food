# Week 1 Launch Plan — Instagram + LinkedIn

**Launch week:** Day 1 (a Monday) to Day 7 (Sunday). Pick the Monday when you're ready.
**Prep week:** the Monday to Sunday right before launch week.

All times are IST. Creative IDs (`IG-01`, `LI-01` and so on) refer to [creatives.md](creatives.md).

## Picking the week

- **Start on a Monday.** LinkedIn is busiest Tuesday to Thursday, and Friday and Saturday nights are the biggest ordering nights. A Monday start puts our strongest posts on those days.
- **Give yourself the full prep week.** 7 days to make the creatives instead of rushing them.
- **Look for a festival or event in launch week or the week after**, and use the house that fits it:

| Occasion | House | Angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diwali, Eid, Christmas, New Year | Dawat Club 🎉 | "Who's hosting this year?" |
| Halloween, exam season, a big late-night match | Midnight Munchies 🌙 | "Your 2 am fridge raid" |
| Monsoon or the first rain | Dal Chawal Gang 🍛 | Chai, pakode, khichdi |
| Holidays, long weekends | Safar Squad 🧭 | "Taste a new city from your couch" |
| January resolutions | Taaza Tribe 🥗 | Fresh and light, without the guilt |

If one falls on Day 6 (Saturday), use it for `IG-08`. If it falls the week after, tease it on Day 7 (`IG-S7`, `IG-09`).

## When to post

These are starting points. After week 1, use Instagram Insights and LinkedIn Analytics to move them.

| Channel | Best slots | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram feed / Reels | **12:30–1:30 pm** and **7:30–9:00 pm** | People scroll at lunch, and 7:30–9 pm is when everyone is stuck deciding what to have for dinner. That's our moment |
| Instagram Stories | 10 am, 1 pm, 8 pm, 11 pm | Stories only last a day. Post a few through the day. The 11 pm slot is for late-night snackers |
| LinkedIn | **Tue–Thu, 8:30–10:00 am**, or 12:30 pm | Professionals check LinkedIn on the commute and at lunch. Weekends are quiet |

**For every LinkedIn post:** stay in the comments for the first 60 minutes and reply to everyone. LinkedIn shows a post to more people when it gets early comments.

## Every day

- **Reply to every comment and DM within 2 hours** (between 9 am and 11 pm).
- **Spend 15 minutes on Instagram interacting with others:** comment on 10 posts from food creators, food pages in our cities, and accounts that tag #swiggy or #foodie.
- **Spend 15 minutes on LinkedIn:** comment thoughtfully on 5–10 posts from founders, product people and food-tech people.
- **At 10 pm:** write the day's numbers in the sheet (see the README).

## What to look at on Sunday night

1. **Which hook won?** Compare saves, shares, and waitlist sign-ups per creative ID: the pain (`IG-02`), identity (`IG-03`), the product (`IG-04`), or play (`IG-05`/`IG-06`).
2. **Which time slot won?** Compare lunch with evening for the feed posts.
3. **LinkedIn:** did the founder's posts bring sign-ups, or only likes?
4. **Week 2:** make two more posts like the winning hook, drop the weakest, and lead with the next festival and its house if one is coming up.
