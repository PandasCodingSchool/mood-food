# Week 1 Creatives

Every creative scheduled in [week-1-plan.md](week-1-plan.md). Each one has an ID used in its UTM tag (`utm_content=<id>`).

## Brand basics for all creatives

- **Look:** use the 2.0 design system (`packages/tokens`, `docs/design/moodfood-2.0`). Colours change with time × weather × mood, so match the creative to the moment (warm evening colours for 8 pm posts, dark and moody for Midnight Munchies).
- **Real product only:** screen-record the actual app on a real phone. Put it in a simple phone frame, not a busy mockup.
- **Food photos:** warm, close-up, real Indian dishes. No glossy stock photos of salads.
- **Type:** big and easy to read. Every slide should make sense in under 2 seconds.
- **Logo:** small, bottom corner. The end card on every Reel shows the logo and "Join the waitlist · link in bio".
- **Captions on every Reel.** Most people watch with the sound off.
- **Safe zones for Reels and Stories:** keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px.

| Format | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram feed / carousel | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) |
| Instagram Reel / Story | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) |
| LinkedIn single image | 1200 × 1500 (4:5) or 1200 × 1200 |
| LinkedIn document (carousel) | PDF, 1080 × 1350 pages |

### IG-02 · Reel — "8 pm vs. 9:15 pm"
**Format:** Reel, 15–20 s, founder or a friend on camera · **Posts:** Mon 8 pm

- **13–18 s:** "Or… 4 taps" → screen recording of MoodFood → 3 picks.
- **End card:** logo + "Join the waitlist".

### IG-03 · Carousel — "Which food house are you?"
**Format:** carousel, 8 slides · **Posts:** Tue 12:30 pm
- **Slide 1:** "There are 6 kinds of eaters. Which one are you? 👇"
- **Slides 2–7:** one per house. Big crest emoji, house name, motto, and 3 funny "you know you're one if…" lines. Examples:
  - **Midnight Munchies 🌙:** "Your Swiggy history is 80% after 11 pm."
  - **Dal Chawal Gang 🍛:** "You judge every restaurant against your mum's dal."
  - **Mirchi Gang 🌶️:** "'Medium spicy' is an insult."
  - **Safar Squad 🧭:** "You've ordered Ethiopian 'just to see'."
  - **Dawat Club 🎉:** "You order for 4 when you're 2."
  - **Taaza Tribe 🥗:** "You actually read the ingredients."
- **Slide 8:** "Tag your gang 👇 · Find out for real, link in bio."
**Why it works:** people share their own result and tag friends.

## LinkedIn

Posts from the founder's profile are written in the first person: short lines, lots of white space, and the first 2 lines have to make people click "…see more". **Waitlist link goes in the first comment.**

### LI-01 · Launch story (founder) · Mon 9 am · image: app hero screenshot
> We got tired of asking "what should we eat?"
>
> Every night: 40 minutes of scrolling, 3 apps open, and we'd order the same thing again.
>
> So we built MoodFood. You tell it how you feel (4 taps or a 30-second game). It looks at the time and the weather, and gives you 3 dishes, each with a reason. Order on Swiggy right there, or cook it with Instamart.
>
> Today we're opening 100 founding-member spots.
>
> If you've ever been in the "kuch bhi chalega… no, not that" loop, this is for you. Link in comments 👇

### LI-02 · "Why we gave our users food houses" (founder) · Tue 8:30 am · image: 6-house grid
The idea: a preference form feels like admin work, but finding out your house feels like getting to know yourself. Explain the design choice (identity makes people want to share it, and it gives the recommender a starting point for your taste). Finish with a question: "What's your house?" That one line brings in comments.

### LI-03 · "How MoodFood picks 3 dishes" (founder) · Wed 9 am · LinkedIn document, 5 pages
1. The problem: too many choices.
2. The inputs: mood + time of day + weather + taste.
3. The output: 3 picks, each with a reason (why 3 and not 10).
4. Getting the food: Swiggy MCP for ordering, Instamart for ingredients.
5. What we will **never** do: use someone's mood to sell to them (link to our principles).
Point 5 builds trust, and few companies say it out loud.

### LI-04 · "3 things we learned designing a food game" (founder) · Thu 8:30 am · text, optional GIF
Only use lessons from real testing. If we don't have data yet, make it about the design ideas behind the game instead (for example: "30 seconds or less, otherwise it becomes a chore"). Never make up numbers.

### LI-05 · "Week 1 so far" (company page) · Fri 12:30 pm · image: stat card
Real numbers: founding members, cities, house split. Thank the people who shared. Tag the founder.
